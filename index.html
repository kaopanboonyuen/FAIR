Mathematical Foundation

Deterministic Validation

FAIR integrates deterministic image quality analysis with semantic automotive verification to establish production-grade visual integrity for underwriting intelligence systems.

Blur Detection

Variance of Laplacian

∇²
$$ \mathcal{L}(I) = \frac{1}{N} \sum_{i=1}^{N} \left( \nabla^2 I_i - \mu_{\nabla^2 I} \right)^2 $$ $$ \text{Pass}_{blur} = \mathbb{1} \left( \mathcal{L}(I) \geq T_{blur} \right) $$

FAIR evaluates high-frequency spatial responses using second-order differential operators to quantify structural sharpness and reject visually degraded evidence prior to downstream AI inference.

FAIR Eligibility Rule

Φ
$$ \texttt{FAIR}(I) = \mathbb{1} \Big( \mathcal{Q}(I) \land \mathcal{S}(I) \land \mathcal{Z}(I) \Big) $$ $$ \mathcal{Q}(I) = \text{Pass}_{blur} \land \text{Pass}_{brightness} \land \text{Pass}_{darkness} $$ $$ \mathcal{S}(I) = \mathbb{1} \left( \hat{y} \in \mathcal{T}_{valid} \right) $$

FAIR formulates visual eligibility as a hierarchical logical inference system combining deterministic quality assurance, semantic automotive validation, and structural context verification into a unified production-grade governance pipeline.
